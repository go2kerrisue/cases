Case Study

When the System Fails,
Start with the People

How a broken LMS became a catalyst for a full organizational change management cycle — and a platform that actually worked.

Organization Integra
Role LMS Administrator & Training Content Developer
Focus LMS Selection & Implementation
Methodology ADKAR Change Management
Investigate → Synthesize → Implement
The Situation

A training team running on a system that was working against them

When Kerri joined Integra, the training team was operating on a Learning Management System built as a plugin for SharePoint. The dedicated programmers who had once maintained it were gone. What remained was a team struggling with persistent software failures, content problems they couldn't resolve, and no clear technical support.

The system wasn't just frustrating — it was actively limiting the team's ability to do their work. But no one had yet built the case to change it.

"The problem wasn't that people didn't know the system was broken. The problem was that nobody had the data — or the mandate — to fix it."

The Approach

A full ADKAR cycle, from diagnosis to adoption

Rather than jump straight to solutions, Kerri started where every good change management process starts: understanding. What followed was a complete change cycle — building awareness, earning buy-in, educating stakeholders, enabling the organization, and reinforcing adoption.

A Awareness

Investigate — Surface the problem with evidence

Kerri facilitated a series of needs analysis and feedback sessions with the training team — then pulled data reports from the existing system to document exactly where it was falling short. Not opinions. Evidence. The reports made the invisible visible and gave leadership something concrete to act on.

D Desire

Investigate — Expand the conversation across the org

With director support, Kerri engaged department leads across the full organization — not just the training team — to understand needs from every angle. This wasn't just data collection. It was the work of building a coalition: people who understood the problem and were ready to support a solution.

K Knowledge

Synthesize — Research, RFP, and vendor selection

Kerri conducted a full market scan, developed evaluation criteria, and ran a formal RFP process that narrowed the field to three finalists. Each vendor presented live demos to the team. The group evaluated together and selected Cornerstone OnDemand — a decision grounded in real criteria, not preference.

A Ability

Implement — Configure, train, and launch

Kerri led the full Cornerstone OnDemand implementation: system configuration, data migration, and hands-on training for the training team. She also developed the onboarding content that would help the rest of the organization navigate the new system — turning technical setup into a learning experience.

R Reinforcement

Implement — Communications strategy for full adoption

The launch wasn't the finish line. Kerri developed a communications strategy to bring the full organization along — setting expectations, supporting adoption, and ensuring the investment in the new system translated into sustained, organization-wide use.

This is the Creative Perspective Detective at work.

Every engagement follows the same core methodology — but the application is always specific to what the organization actually needs. At Integra, that meant recognizing that a technology problem was really a change management problem in disguise.

The Outcome

A system the organization could actually use — and trust

The Cornerstone OnDemand implementation gave Integra a modern, stable learning platform with real reporting capabilities and genuine organizational adoption. But the bigger outcome was structural: the training team now had a system that supported their work instead of fighting it, and the full organization had been brought along through a thoughtful, human-centered process.

What this engagement delivered

  • Documented needs assessment surfacing gaps across the training function and multiple departments
  • Formal RFP process with defined evaluation criteria and competitive vendor selection
  • Full Cornerstone OnDemand implementation — configuration, data migration, content development
  • Training program for the training team on the new platform
  • Organization-wide communications and onboarding strategy
  • Reporting framework to track training effectiveness and present results to leadership
